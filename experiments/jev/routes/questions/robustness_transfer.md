# robustness transfer

100 questions: 10 proposed routes, each examined from 10 angles. All answers are exploratory judgments until measured.

## use past jobs only when they are relevant

Jev selects reference jobs for strategy transfer from frozen summaries, with family-separated evaluation.

**Evidence needed:** Untouched eligible families, declared perturbations, frozen controller and measured transfer performance.

- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_speed_2x** — Could we use past jobs only when they are relevant and reach the same useful nesting quality in half the time?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_speed_5x** — Could we use past jobs only when they are relevant and cut the time to a useful nest by five times?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_material** — If we use past jobs only when they are relevant, could we use meaningfully less material in the same search time?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_jev_value** — If we use past jobs only when they are relevant, would Jev make it faster than a cheap rule making those same decisions?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_evidence_ready** — Do our current records contain enough information to tell Jev when to use past jobs only when they are relevant?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_ask_early** — Would asking Jev before the expensive work be the better moment to decide whether to use past jobs only when they are relevant?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_history_helps** — Would showing what has already worked and failed help Jev decide whether to use past jobs only when they are relevant?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_parallel_batch** — Can we ask about several independent opportunities to use past jobs only when they are relevant together and make the whole search faster?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_transfer** — If it helps to use past jobs only when they are relevant here, is that likely to survive on unfamiliar jobs with different shapes and quantities?
- **robustness_transfer_job_similarity_bad_failure** — Could a confident but wrong decision to use past jobs only when they are relevant leave us with a seriously worse nest?
